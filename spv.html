② Verify a transaction is in a block — SPV

An SPV client confirms a payment with a tiny merkle proof plus the block header — no full block, no full chain. Here we take a real testnet4 block (#26,000), build a BIP37 proof for one transaction, and verify it (proof-of-work, a well-formed tree, the root matching the header, and the tx included). In a real wallet the proof comes from a peer; we build it here to show the check.
