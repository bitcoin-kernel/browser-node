bitcoin-kernel / browser-node — mempool watch (testnet4)

Watch the unconfirmed mempool for payments to an address — in your browser, with no explorer API. This tab connects to a real testnet4 peer over the bridge, listens to the transaction relay flow (inv → getdata → tx), decodes each transaction itself, and flags any output paying your address. Funds you catch here can be spent in the wallet. how it works · watch-only

⚠ It watches the flow, it can't dump the pool. The reachable testnet4 peers don't serve BIP37 bloom or BIP157 compact filters (services 0x409/0xc09), so there's no "give me everything paying address X" query. This catches payments as they propagate to us while the tab is open — so open it, then trigger the payment. A payment already broadcast and finished propagating may not reappear until it confirms (then the scan finds it).
